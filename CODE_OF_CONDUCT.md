# Code of Conduct

GrandTour is a small project and we want it to stay a pleasant one to work
on. Everyone who participates here, whether in issues, pull requests,
discussions, or reviews, agrees to the following.

## Expected

- Be direct and be kind. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Ask before concluding.
- Respect people's time: keep issues and PRs focused, and say what you
  tested.
- Welcome newcomers. Point to the docs; don't gatekeep.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or lock contributions that violate this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who keeps violating it. Report
problems to the maintainers privately via
[GitHub's report feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or by opening a private security advisory on this repository. Reports are
handled confidentially.
