# GrandTour PRD

URL: https://GrandTour.fyi

## Vision

GrandTour is a configurable, location-aware audio platform that automatically delivers contextual commentary as users move through physical space.

The world speaks to you through the layers you choose.

## Core Ideas

- GPS-triggered audio commentary
- Walking, driving, cycling, hiking, museum, transit, boating, and aviation modes
- Community-created content
- Professional guides
- Celebrity creators
- AI-generated content
- Plugin/layer architecture
- Human-guide-first philosophy

## Content Layers

Examples:

- History
- Architecture
- Restaurants
- Real Estate
- Nature
- Museums
- Shopping
- Transportation
- Aviation
- Public Safety
- Local Events
- Creator-specific layers
- Any future place-based layer

Users enable desired layers and hear relevant tracks automatically as they travel.

## Human Guides, Not Replacement

GrandTour is designed to promote and generate leads for local guides.

The platform should:
- Promote local experts
- Encourage direct bookings
- Include guide profiles
- Provide booking/contact links
- Surface human-created content whenever available

Success is measured partly by successful guide connections.

## Creator Platform

Anyone should be able to contribute.

### Walk and Record

A creator can:

1. Open GrandTour
2. Press Record
3. Walk, drive, bike, hike, boat, or fly
4. Narrate stories
5. Publish

The system records:
- Audio
- GPS
- Heading
- Speed
- Time

### Map Authoring

Creators can:

- Drop markers
- Draw routes
- Define trigger regions
- Upload audio
- Generate AI narration
- Create complete tours remotely

### Desktop Studio

Advanced editing:
- Multi-track editing
- Timeline editing
- Analytics
- Publishing tools
